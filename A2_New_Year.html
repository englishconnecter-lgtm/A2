New Year Celebrations

Talk about New Year’s Eve and share your resolutions.

How did you celebrate?

Listen to two friends compare their plans for the new year and how they spent New Year’s Eve.

LevelA2
Time45–50 minutes
SkillsListening & Speaking

Talk about New Year’s Eve

Lead-in questions

  1. How did you celebrate New Year’s Eve?
  2. Do you prefer to celebrate at home or at a party? Why?
  3. What time did you go to sleep?

Useful phrases

We stayed up until midnight.

We watched the fireworks.

I want to read more this year.

First listen

New Year’s Eve conversation

True or false?

Listen to Emma and David and decide whether each statement is true or false.

1. Emma celebrated New Year’s Eve at a friend’s house.
2. Emma’s family had a big dinner with special food.
3. David went to a party with about twenty people.
4. Emma and David both watched fireworks on TV.
5. Emma went to bed before midnight.
6. David went to sleep at three o’clock in the morning.
7. One of David’s New Year’s resolutions is to learn to cook.
8. Emma wants to spend more time on her phone.

Second listen

Listen for details

Listen again and choose the correct answer.

1. What did Emma’s mum make for dinner?
2. What did David do at the party?
3. Why did Emma watch the fireworks on TV?
4. What time did Emma go to bed?
5. What happened to Emma’s little brother?
6. What is Emma’s New Year’s resolution?

Past simple and resolutions

Past continuous

Use was / were + verb-ing for an action in progress at a time in the past.

At midnight, we were watching the fireworks.

Emma was sleeping when the new year began.

For an action in progress interrupted by another event, use past continuous + when + past simple.

We were dancing when the clock struck twelve.

Past simple for celebrations

Positive We watched fireworks. We went to a party.

Negative I didn’t watch fireworks.

Question Did you watch fireworks?

Use the base form after did and didn’t.

Past simple or past continuous?

Choose the correct form. Use the past continuous for an action in progress and the past simple for a completed event.

1. At midnight, we ___ the fireworks when the clock struck twelve.
2. Emma ___ a chocolate cake for the party.
3. While David ___, his friends played music.
4. Emma’s little brother ___ asleep before midnight.
5. It ___ freezing outside, so Emma’s family watched the fireworks on TV.
6. David ___ home at three o’clock in the morning.
7. The guests ___ games while the music was playing.
8. Emma ___ to bed around one o’clock.

New Year interview

Interview a partner

Take turns asking questions. Ask one follow-up question about each answer.

  1. Where did you spend New Year’s Eve?
  2. Who did you celebrate with?
  3. What did you eat or drink?
  4. What did you do at midnight?
  5. What time did you go to bed?

Share a resolution

Tell your partner one thing you want to do this year. Ask your partner what they want to do. Agree on a month to check your progress.
