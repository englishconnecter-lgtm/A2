Making a Complaint

Explain a problem and ask for help politely.

What would you say at the shop?

Listen to a customer return a laptop. Then practise asking for a refund or replacement.

LevelA2
Time50–60 minutes
SkillsListening & Speaking

Before you listen

Discuss with a partner

  1. Have you ever taken something back to a shop? What happened?
  2. What would you do if a new laptop stopped working?
  3. What can you say when you need help with a problem?

Shopping and returns

Returns and complaints

Receipt A piece of paper or an email that shows you paid.

Refund Money a shop gives back to you.

Replacement Another item the shop gives you in place of the old one.

Warranty A promise to repair or replace an item if it develops a problem within a set time.

In stock Available to buy now.

Work properly Work the way it should.

Choose the word

Choose the best word or phrase for each situation.

1. The shop gives you your money back.
2. The shop gives you another laptop.
3. You need to prove you paid for the laptop.
4. The shop has the same model available today.
5. The shop promises to repair the product if it develops a fault in the first year.

Polite complaints and requests

Polite complaints and requests

I’m having problems with + noun. I’m having problems with this laptop.

Could I have + noun, please? Could I have a refund, please?

Could you + verb, please? Could you check the receipt, please?

I’d like / I’d prefer + noun. I’d prefer a replacement, please.

Choose the more polite phrase

Choose a phrase for each situation.

1. Ask for your money back.
2. Explain a problem with your order.
3. Ask to speak to the manager.
4. Respond to a complaint.

Role-play a complaint

Work in pairs. One person is the customer and the other is the employee. Choose a situation, agree on a solution, then swap roles.

Restaurant

Customer You ordered a vegetarian meal, but it has chicken in it. You want a new meal.

Employee Apologize and offer to bring a new meal immediately for free.

Clothing Store

Customer You bought a shirt yesterday. When you got home, you found a hole in it. You want a refund.

Employee Check the receipt. Offer a replacement or refund.

Hotel

Customer Your hotel room is very noisy. You can't sleep. You want to change rooms.

Employee Apologize. Check if there are quieter rooms available.

Electronics Store

Customer You bought headphones last week. The sound only works in one ear. You want a replacement.

Employee Test the headphones. Offer a replacement with a new warranty.

Coffee Shop

Customer You ordered a hot coffee, but it's cold. You want a new one.

Employee Apologize and make a fresh hot coffee immediately.

Bookstore

Customer You bought a book online. It arrived damaged. You want a replacement.

Employee Apologize. Send a new book and provide a return label for the damaged one.

Write a complaint

Write an email to customer service

Explain what you bought, when you bought it and what went wrong. Ask for a refund, a replacement or a repair. Use a greeting and a polite closing.

Show a model email

Dear Customer Service,

I bought a pair of headphones from your shop last week. The sound only works in one ear. Could you send me a replacement, please? I can send you a copy of my receipt.

Thank you for your help.

Kind regards,
Alex
