Prep: The morning rush!

Two colleagues. One busy morning. Who gets to work on time?

Complete the mini-story using the word bank. Use each answer once. Then compare your answers with a partner.

Word bank: membership · catch · late · motivates · ride · direct debit · take · blows out

1. It is 7:30! Mia puts on her helmet. “I usually to work. My bike is faster than the bus!”

2. Ben runs to the station. “Hurry! We need to the train at 7:45!”

3. “How long does your journey ?” Mia asks. “About twenty minutes,” says Ben.

4. Oh no! Ben cannot find his keys and misses his train. His morning — everything takes longer than planned!

5. Mia goes to the gym before work. She has a gym , so she can exercise there every day.

6. The gym takes money from her bank account automatically each month by .

7. “Paying every month me to go,” Mia says. “I want to use what I pay for!”

8. At 8:30, Mia arrives at the office. Ben arrives at 9:10. “Sorry I’m — I missed my train!”

Language tip: “My morning blows out” is an informal way to say that your morning takes longer than expected.

Your turn: Who has a morning like yours: Mia or Ben? Tell your partner one thing that can make you late.

Discussion

  1. How do you usually get to school or work?
  2. How long does it take?
  3. What time do you leave home?
  4. What happens if you leave later than usual?
  5. What do you do when it is raining?
  6. Do you prefer driving, cycling, walking or public transport?
  7. Is commuting a good time to relax, exercise or work?

Role Play: Morning Small Talk

Situation: Two colleagues meet at work and talk about their commute.

Student A

Ask about transport, travel time and rainy days. React naturally to your partner’s answers.

Student B

Answer the questions and ask Student A about their commute. Add one problem with your journey.
