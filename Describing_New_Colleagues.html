Describing people

Be, have and wear

Be + adjective Tom is tall. Maria is patient.

Have / has + noun phrase She has long dark hair. Put length or shape before colour.

Wear / wears Mr. Johnson wears glasses.

Talking about age

Use be with age. Lisa is 22. Mr. Johnson is about 50.

In her twenties means 20–29. Use thirties for 30–39, forties for 40–49 and fifties for 50–59.

Describe your colleague

Describe an imaginary colleague

  1. Choose a name, an age and a job. Add three details about appearance and three personality traits.
  2. Describe your colleague. Your partner takes notes or draws a simple picture, then asks a follow-up question.
  3. Swap roles.

Useful phrases

She’s in her thirties. He has short blond hair. She’s very patient. He’s good at solving problems.

Write a profile

Write a short description

Describe a real or imaginary colleague, classmate or family member. Include their age, appearance, personality and something they’re good at. Aim for 100–120 words.
