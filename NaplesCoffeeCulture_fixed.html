A2 Grammar: There is / There are + Present Simple

Use there is and there are to describe places and traditions. Use the present simple for habits.

FormExample
There is + singular nounThere is a coffee bar in the piazza.
There are + plural nounThere are many cafés in Naples.
People + present simple verbPeople drink coffee every day.
They + present simple verbThey meet friends at the bar.
